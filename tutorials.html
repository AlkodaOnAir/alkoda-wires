2 possible views

Front and rear views of your devices

Starting with Wires 1.5, devices can have both a Front and a Rear view, each with its own connection points.

Connect cables from one side to the other: Wires hides the portions that run behind the device, and a cable already in place moves from Front to Rear without being recreated.

Image search

Find your device directly.

Quickly find the devices you need with the collaborative image library built into Wires.

With more than 3,000 products and 10,000 images available, search for a model, choose the view that best fits your diagram, then prepare the image and add connection points directly on the device's physical connectors.

And if a product is not yet available, searching for it can help add it to the database and make it available to everyone.

Wireless connections

Connect your devices wirelessly.

See how Wires handles wireless connections such as WiFi, Bluetooth and RF directly inside your projects.

Add wireless connection points to your devices, create the connection and include it in a signal route alongside your wired connections. Then animate the route to clearly see the signal travel from one device to another, including across the wireless link.

Signal routes

Animate all your signal routes.

See all signal routes in a Wires project animate at the same time, giving you a clear overview of the entire signal flow.

Group cables into routes and follow each signal from source to destination. Animate the whole installation at once, or isolate a single route to explain a chain, spot a break, and see how the signal passes through converters, switches and displays.
